About me

Hands-on IT, from server room to sales floor.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from STI College Caloocan. From 2016 to 2024 I was a Technical Support specialist at PPI Holdings Inc., which operates Pizza Hut, Taco Bell and Dairy Queen in the Philippines. My job was to keep stores, call centres and data centres running.

I set up and troubleshot POS systems across five brands and led migrations from Fenix to Cuscapi, from Cuscapi to Aloha, and from Aloha and Chase to ANSI. I also installed structured cabling, built Windows servers with daily backups, and maintained Avaya and PABX telephony, CCTV, biometric HRIS and door-lock systems. For new store openings, I handled the full IT setup.

Since moving to Québec City, I've worked in restaurant and retail operations, including as Assistant Restaurant General Manager. That work taught me how the systems I used to support hold up during a busy shift. In parallel I build with PHP, JavaScript, MySQL and Lua, and I have been a FiveM developer since 2019, adding RedM game servers in 2025. I also work as an AI engineer, connecting large language models to real business tools: chat assistants, automated support workflows, and features built on AI APIs.

Mavarne RedM scripts

A suite of RedM resources I write and maintain under the Mavarne name, built on VORP Core with Lua, oxmysql and custom NUI interfaces. They are designed to work together: Priority Status gates robberies, and the gameplay scripts share the same progress bar and notification style.

mavarne_cockfighting

Cockfighting Pits

Raise, train and breed roosters, then fight them in PvP matches with stakes and spectator betting. The server decides every fight, bet, payout and breeding roll.

  • Chicks hatch at the ranch and grow into fighters; up to 6 roosters per character
  • Care that keeps running offline: hunger, energy, grooming, sickness, wounds and bleeding
  • 9 stats capped by genetic potential and rarity, with XP and levels
  • Breeding with rising fees, a 7-breed limit and no close relatives; eggs hatch in your coop
  • PvP fights with equal stakes, spectator betting, a live fight HUD and practice spars
  • Losers can die or carry injuries that need medicine to heal
  • Rooster auction house, player-to-player sales and a feed and medicine dealer
  • Rooster book (/manok) and a leaderboard
  • VORP
  • oxmysql
  • NUI
  • Genetics

  • Teleport to backpacks and houses, toggle house blips, edit or revive horses
  • Delete mode (/admindelete) locked to a server-side Steam allowlist
  • Bucket and visibility fixes: /bucket, /fixbucket, /checkplayer, /fixvisible, /vislog
  • Discord logs for txAdmin actions, /heal, /revive, /tpm, money and item spawns

mavarne_society

Society Job System

A VORP-native job system with an old-RedM styled interface. It replaces both vorp_billing and vorp_paycheck and creates its own database schema on first start.

  • Admin panel (/jobsadmin) to create societies, ranks and paycheck amounts
  • Boss Actions (/boss): hire, promote, society balance, deposit and withdraw
  • Bosses can only manage ranks below their own
  • Ranked paychecks paid by the server or the society treasury
  • Time clock (/duty) with live shift timer and shift history
  • Public and personal society storage, opened with a G prompt
  • Billing (/bill) with income flowing into the treasury
